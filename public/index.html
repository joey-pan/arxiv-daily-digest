Localizing Concepts in Visual Autoregressive Models

视觉自回归模型中的概念定位

Nanxiang Jiang, Yang Liu, Yuanhao Wang, Min Xu

核心贡献: 本文提出了LoCo，首个与模型无关的框架，用于定位自回归模型中特定概念出现的位置和时机，并构建了LoCoBench数据集以系统评估概念路由。
方法: 利用下一尺度生成固有的由粗到细特性，该方法从层、尺度、位置三个维度精确映射概念知识。为消除上下文偏差，作者构建了涵盖10个类别的LoCoBench数据集，并在Infinity、HunyuanImage-3.0和InfinityStar等图像与视频自回归模型上进行了广泛探测。基于定位结果，方法被应用于概念擦除、模型个性化和对抗性概念注入三个场景。
关键发现: 实验表明，定位到的位置具有可解释性且与概念涌现存在因果关系；基于该定位的定向干预在概念擦除、个性化和对抗注入任务上达到最先进性能，同时大幅降低计算开销并保持良性效用，为更可解释、高效和安全的模型适配提供了实用路径。

Understanding the internal routing of visual concepts is crucial for the safe and controllable adaptation of generative models. While concept localization has been widely studied in diffusion models, the emerging paradigm of next-scale visual autoregressive models remains largely unexplored. In this paper, we introduce LoCo, the first model-agnostic framework to localize where and when specific concepts emerge within autoregressive models. Driven by the native coarse-to-fine nature of next-scale generation, our method precisely maps conceptual knowledge across three distinct dimensions: Layer, Scale, and Position. To systematically localize and evaluate concept routing without context bias, we propose LoCoBench, a comprehensive dataset spanning 10 diverse categories. Extensive probing on image and video autoregressive models like Infinity, HunyuanImage-3.0, and InfinityStar shows that the localized positions are both interpretable and causally related to concept emergence. Building on these insights, we apply our localization method to three key applications: concept erasure, model personalization, and adversarial concept injection. Experiments demonstrate that our targeted intervention achieves state-of-the-art performance, substantially reducing computational overhead while preserving benign utility. Overall, our findings offer insights into how conceptual knowledge is routed during autoregressive generation, introducing a practical pathway for more interpretable, efficient, and secure adaptation.

MorphSIG: Subject-Driven Image Generation via Decoupled Anchoring and Feature Transport

MorphSIG：通过解耦锚定与特征传输的主体驱动图像生成

Hailong Yan, Yongrui Zhang, Xiangtao Zhang, Le Zhang

核心贡献: 提出MorphSIG，一个无需训练的框架，将主体驱动图像生成重新表述为伪视频特征传输，在无需额外训练的情况下显著提升姿态多样性和主体保真度。
方法: MorphSIG包含两个核心模块：渐进式解耦锚定模块，在早期去噪阶段利用频域解耦和结构扰乱来打破结构锁定，实现从自由构图到身份对齐的逐步过渡；伪流引导特征传输模块，构建潜在空间伪流场以模拟图像到视频的时间一致性，并精确传输主体语义。此外，引入了一个包含多样风格化主体的基准测试。
关键发现: 大量实验表明，MorphSIG在无需额外训练的情况下，相比基线显著提升了姿态多样性和主体保真度，弥合了静态生成与动态传播之间的差距，为高保真主体一致性提供了一种经济有效的范式。
查看原文摘要

We propose MorphSIG, a training-free framework that reformulates subject-driven image generation (SIG) as pseudo-video feature transport. MorphSIG contains two core modules: Progressive Decoupled Anchoring, which uses frequency-domain decoupling and structural scrambling during early denoising to break structural locking and enable a gradual transition from free composition to identity alignment; and Pseudo-flow Guided Feature Transport, which constructs a latent-space pseudo-flow field to mimic Image-to-Video temporal coherence and transport subject semantics precisely. To address stylistic homogeneity and the lack of ground truth in existing benchmarks, we further introduce a benchmark with diverse stylized subjects. Extensive experiments show that MorphSIG significantly improves pose diversity and subject fidelity over baselines without additional training. Overall, MorphSIG bridges static generation and dynamic propagation, providing a cost-effective paradigm for high-fidelity subject consistency.

Calibrating Attention-Based Token Pruning for Efficient Multi-Image Synthesis in UMMs

校准基于注意力的Token剪枝以实现统一多模态模型中高效的多图像合成

Zimeng Wu, Qijian Tian, Penghui Du, Changqian Yu, Jiaxin Chen 等 (6 位作者)

核心贡献: 提出了CATP，首个专为统一多模态模型（UMM）生成任务设计的免训练上下文压缩方法，通过校准通用注意力Token剪枝中的偏差，在多图像合成中实现更优的质量-效率权衡。
方法: 在去噪过程中逐层独立剪枝的基础上，引入引导一致性剪枝（GCP）以同步条件分支间的剪枝决策，并提出泰勒校准注意力度量（TCAM），从理论上推导出针对池化引入偏差的二阶校正。此外，设计快速评分算子（FSO）将校准融合到分块在线Softmax中以降低剪枝开销。

Unified Multimodal Models (UMMs) enable powerful multi-image synthesis but incur substantial computation from long visual contexts. While token pruning offers a direct route to accelerating inference, existing methods are primarily adapted from understanding tasks, suffering from insufficient reference image exploration, confused conditional guidance, and pooling-induced ambiguity that degrades generation quality. To address these issues, we propose CATP, the first training-free context-compression method tailored to UMM generation, which \textbf{C}alibrates the discrepancies of general \textbf{A}ttention-based \textbf{T}oken \textbf{P}runing for better quality-efficiency trade-off. Concretely, building on independent layer-wise pruning during denoising, we introduce Guidance-Consistent Pruning (GCP) to synchronize pruning decisions across conditioning branches, and a Taylor-Calibrated Attention Metric (TCAM) that derives a theoretically grounded second-order correction for pooling-induced bias. In addition, a Fast Scoring Operator (FSO) fuses the calibration into tiled online Softmax to reduce pruning cost. Extensive experiments across representative UMMs and benchmarks demonstrate that CATP consistently outperforms state-of-the-art token-reduction methods, achieving up to 2.09$\times$ attention-core speedup.

Visual commissions require image agents to integrate heterogeneous source materials, preserve requirements across dependent stages, and produce coordinated deliverables. Existing evaluations examine specific capabilities and execution settings, leaving end-to-end commission completion less understood. We introduce Visual Commission Bench (VCB), comprising 600 tasks across 12 categories and three complementary tiers, from capability-focused tasks to complete commissions. VCB uses 5,039 requirement checks and separate visual quality ratings to assess content correctness, visual realization, constraint preservation, and delivery completeness without prescribing an internal workflow. We present V-Protocol, a reference agent that dynamically composes reusable task, cognitive, and expression modules at inference time. Task modules organize delivery, cognitive modules derive content, and expression modules guide visual realization. The modules exchange structured intermediate artifacts, while a shared execution harness tracks artifact revisions and dependencies, manages tool execution, and checks completion. With the same controller and image generator as the agent baselines, V-Protocol achieves 69.0\% strict task success on VCB, compared with 42.0\% for SCOPE. On Mind-Bench, strict accuracy increases from 39.2\% to 45.4\%.

Unlocking Any-Order Generation in Pretrained Autoregressive Image Models

解锁预训练自回归图像模型的任意顺序生成能力

Rishav Pramanik, Marco Pedersoli, Zhaozheng Yin

核心贡献: 论文提出一种低成本适配方案，将预训练的栅格扫描自回归图像模型转化为支持任意顺序生成的模型，从而在不重新训练、不改变标准AR推理栈的前提下实现图像修复、外扩和编辑等灵活生成能力。
方法: 作者认为预训练栅格扫描AR模型已学到图像token的联合分布，缺的只是通过非顺序条件路径表达该知识的能力。为此提出两个组件：一是未来感知位置嵌入，以极小开销解决目标位置可识别性问题；二是顺序课程学习，引导模型从预训练解稳定优化到良好的任意顺序解空间。该方法应用于LlamaGen-L/XL和RAR-L/XL四个基座模型进行验证。
关键发现: 在ImageNet图像修复任务上，该方法FID为6.21，显著优于MaskGIT的10.44，并在图像外扩任务上同样表现优异；与MAR-L相比具有竞争力，仅以类别条件FID增加1.2-1.9为代价换取灵活性；额外计算开销仅为预训练的13-33%，且完全兼容KV缓存、vLLM等标准AR推理生态。结果表明，自回归图像模型的修复、外扩和编辑能力无需额外添加，可直接从现有模型中解锁。
查看原文摘要

Autoregressive (AR) image generators are powerful in principle but inflexible in practice: training with a fixed raster-scan order hardcodes a single factorization of the image distribution into the model, leaving it unable to inpaint, outpaint, or edit at inference time. Existing solutions, including the modern discrete-diffusion family, train from scratch on a mixture of token orderings, paying roughly 3x the compute of standard raster-scan training; discrete diffusion further sacrifices compatibility with the AR inference ecosystem (KV caching, vLLM) by construction. We argue that this cost is unnecessary. A pretrained raster-scan AR model has already learned the joint distribution over image tokens; what it lacks is not knowledge but the ability to express that knowledge through non-sequential conditional pathways. We propose a recipe for adapting pretrained raster-scan models into any-order generators using two components: future-aware positional embeddings that resolve a target-position identifiability problem at negligible overhead, and an ordering curriculum that traces a stable optimization path from the pretrained solution to a good any-order basin. Applied to four base models across two architectures and two scales (LlamaGen-L/XL, RAR-L/XL), the recipe substantially outperforms MaskGIT on ImageNet inpainting (FID 6.21 vs. 10.44) and outpainting, is competitive with MAR-L, trades a 1.2-1.9 increase in class-conditional FID for this flexibility, and costs 13-33% additional compute beyond pretraining, while leaving the standard AR inference stack untouched. Inpainting, outpainting, and editing, our results suggest, need not be added to autoregressive image models. They can be unlocked from the ones that exist. Code is available at https://github.com/Ordered-AR/unlockedadaptation.
