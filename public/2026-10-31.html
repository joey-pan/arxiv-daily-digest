EditAgent: Rethinking Agentic Image Editing

EditAgent：重新思考智能体图像编辑

Haiyang Xu, Tianyu Wang, Jialu Li, Jiteng Mu, Yuanjun Xiong 等 (8 位作者)

核心贡献: 论文指出智能体图像编辑器受限于动作空间和观察空间两个维度，提出EditAgent通过扩展这两个空间来提升编辑能力，并在多个基准上改进开源编辑器，同时可作为训练更强编辑器的数据引擎。
方法: EditAgent将每个编辑步骤路由到通用编辑器和专用方法，并为每个候选结果附加验证记录；借助这些记录，现成的VLM评判器可优于微调奖励模型。候选结果并行生成，并通过一次listwise调用统一评判，从而在减少评判调用次数的同时提升效果。
关键发现: 在专家标注基准上，通用编辑器超过40%的情况下无法给出可用结果，且没有评判器能在所有编辑类型上可靠工作。专家在78.5%的有判定比较中更偏好该并行生成加listwise评判的方式而非顺序精炼，且评判调用不超过后者一半。EditAgent在三个基准上改进了两个开源权重编辑器。

Agentic image editors decompose complex requests into steps, but when a step fails, they mostly retry with the same narrow set of tools and a judge that scores each output by eye directly. We argue that such agents are bounded by two spaces: the action space of what the agent can do, and the observation space of what its judge can see. On our expert-annotated benchmark, a general-purpose editor yields no usable result in over 40% of the cases, and no judge is reliable across edit types. We present EditAgent, which expands both spaces. It routes each step to general editors and specialized methods, and attaches to each candidate a verification record. With these records, off-the-shelf VLM judges outperform finetuned reward models. Candidates are generated in parallel and judged in one listwise call: experts prefer this to sequential refinement in 78.5% of decided comparisons, at no more than half the judge calls. EditAgent improves two open-weight editors on three benchmarks and serves as a data engine for training stronger editors.

ROAD: Modern Adversarial Training for Simple One-Step Distillation at Scale

ROAD：面向大规模简单一步蒸馏的现代对抗训练

Xiaoou Li, Shan You, Tao Huang

核心贡献: 提出ROAD框架，仅用一个对抗目标和轻量级表示空间判别器，即可将现代文本到图像扩散模型蒸馏为高质量的一步生成器，大幅降低训练复杂度和计算量。
方法: 采用单阶段对抗蒸馏框架，将经典卷积判别头替换为具有token级文本条件控制的Lipschitz约束Transformer头，以匹配现代视觉编码器的语义token；针对固定随机特征混合导致的长程退化问题，周期性重新抽取混合以保持随机化有效性；并引入Jacobian R1正则项，惩罚完整的输入-对数几率雅可比矩阵，克服池化R1忽略大部分对数几率敏感性的局限。
关键发现: 在COCO-10K上，ROAD一步生成在SDXL上达到11.03 FID，在SD3.5-M上达到13.73 FID，以远低于分布匹配的训练计算量超越了多步教师模型。
查看原文摘要

One-step distillation turns pretrained diffusion models into fast generators, but strong results often require substantial training machinery and compute. Pure adversarial distillation offers a simpler route, yet has struggled at modern text-to-image scale. To address this limitation, we propose ROAD (Representation-grounded One-step Adversarial Distillation), a one-stage framework that distills text-to-image models with one adversarial objective and a lightweight representation-space critic. First, we replace the classical convolutional head with a Lipschitz-controlled Transformer head with token-level text conditioning, which matches the semantic tokens of modern visual encoders. Second, we observed a fixed random feature mixing leads to long-horizon degradation. We attribute it to generator-critic co-adaptation around one realization-specific view, and periodically re-draw the mixing to keep the randomization effective. Third, for our dense and vector-valued logits, we introduce Jacobian R1 which penalizes the full input-logit Jacobian, overcoming pooled R1's limitation of neglecting most of logit sensitivities. A single adversarial objective then suffices: in one step, ROAD reaches 11.03 FID on SDXL and 13.73 on SD3.5-M on COCO-10K, surpassing the multi-step teachers at a fraction of distribution matching's training compute.

The central bottleneck in visual generation and editing has migrated from achieving pixel-level fidelity to satisfying densely constrained user specifications through increasingly agentic workflows. Prevailing agentic generation pipelines remain shallow generate-verify-rewrite loops treating VLM judgments as coarse visual feedback, either discarding repair experience or admitting ungated updates that poison skill library. With memorize and govern periods left open, they degrade into ungoverned iterators of repeated trial-and-error rather than continual self-evolving harness. VEvo-Harness surpasses state-of-the-art agentic methods and rivals proprietary generators (\textit{e.g.}, GPT-Image, Nano-Pro) while maintaining the continual harness evolution and generalization.

TokenRepel：在不牺牲质量的前提下生成多样化的图像集

Qingtao Yu, Zhen Wang, Ning Lai, Boyang Hu, Hongdong Li 等 (6 位作者)

核心贡献: 提出一种推理时方法，通过利用预训练模型隐藏表示的内在几何结构，在不降低平均图像质量的情况下增强文本到图像模型生成图像集的视觉多样性。
方法: 首先，引入token排斥机制，在单位超球面上对token的von Mises-Fisher混合分布采取负黎曼梯度步，增大不同样本间特征token的夹角。其次，提出token密度保持机制，将排斥引起的预测变化投影到与模型得分方向对齐的半空间上，使更新保持在生成模型学习到的高密度区域内。进一步将该机制扩展到每个中间层的输出，实现更细粒度的迭代引导。
关键发现: 在三种预训练文本到图像模型上的实验表明，在相同提示下视觉多样性显著提升，同时保持与基线相当的图像保真度。

We present an inference-time approach that enhances the visual diversity of image sets generated by text-to-image models, without compromising the average image quality. Our method leverages the intrinsic geometry of hidden representations in pretrained models to diversify images generated from the same prompt. First, we introduce a token repulsion mechanism that increases the angle between feature tokens across samples. It takes a negative Riemannian gradient step on a von Mises-Fisher mixture distribution of tokens on the unit hypersphere. Second, to preserve the quality of each output image, we propose a token density preservation mechanism that projects the repulsion-induced prediction change onto a half-space aligned with the model's score direction. This encourages the updates to remain within the high-density regions learned by the generative model. We further extend this mechanism to the output of each intermediate layer, enabling finer-grained iterative guidance. We evaluate our method on three types of pretrained text-to-image models. Experiments show substantial gains in visual diversity under identical prompts, while maintaining a comparable level of image fidelity.

We present PhysInsert, a framework for physically plausible reference-based object insertion that jointly adapts objects and their surrounding scenes. Existing methods often rely on masked inputs, generation-based pairs, or training pairs with limited viewpoint variation, leaving pose and illumination adaptation and bidirectional object-scene interactions insufficiently constrained. We construct the Insert World dataset with strictly aligned Blender-rendered counterfactual pairs and object references across viewpoints and lighting conditions. Building on these data, Geometry-Guided Adaptation couples a VGGT geometry stream with image and text streams under target-scene feature supervision to guide spatially consistent insertion. Complementary 2D Object-Scene Spatial Supervision jointly supervises object occupancy and environmental changes while grounding spatial instructions in object regions through image-text attention. We introduce PhysInsert-Bench with dedicated cross-environment and cross-view cases requiring illumination and pose adaptation beyond appearance copying. Experiments on PhysInsert-Bench and DreamBooth demonstrate improved image fidelity; a user study further shows more accurate placement and improved lighting integration, perspective, and pose plausibility.
