SolarRenderer: Few-Step Generative Reference Rendering

SolarRenderer：少步生成式参考渲染

Guian Fang, Shengju Qian, Dongyue Lu, Junchao Huang, Lutao Jiang 等 (9 位作者)

核心贡献: 提出一种结构条件视频渲染器SolarRenderer，通过两个轻量LoRA适配冻结的MiniMax-H3 33B骨干网络，实现仅需四步的高效渲染，并具备对多种未见结构表示的强泛化能力。
方法: 将结构视频以纯文本描述、无类型标识的方式输入骨干网络原生的参考通路，使单一控制LoRA可跟随任意结构表示；从预训练的四步LoRA出发，采用结构引导的分布匹配实现四步渲染；并设计块因果变体以支持流式实时渲染768p视频；训练数据为从游戏引擎、模拟器和YouTube构建的136K帧对齐片段语料。
关键发现: 四步渲染比50步采样快12.3倍且保持所学控制能力；在分布内测试集和包含16种结构表示的泛化基准上均达到最先进水平，并成功泛化到11种未见表示，包括姿态骨架、手绘草图和点云。

Game engines and coding agents can specify the layout, motion, and camera of a scene, yet turning such coarse structure into realistic video requires a renderer that follows the structure and completes the appearance it leaves open. Existing renderers are tied to the representation of one engine, need tens of denoising steps or a costly distillation of their own, and learn from conditions that are not aligned with their target videos. We present SolarRenderer, a structure-conditioned video renderer that adapts a frozen MiniMax-H3 33B backbone with two lightweight LoRAs. Structural videos enter the native reference pathway of the backbone at the positions of the frames they describe, without a type identifier and described only in plain text, so that a single control LoRA follows arbitrary structural representations. Starting from a pretrained four-step LoRA, structure-guided distribution matching renders in four steps, 12.3 times faster than 50-step sampling, while preserving the learned control. A block-causal variant extends the same design to streaming and renders 768p video in real time. To train the model, we build a frame-aligned corpus of 136K clips from game engines, simulators, and YouTube videos. Experiments on an in-distribution test set and a generalization benchmark of 16 structural representations show that SolarRenderer achieves state-of-the-art results across both, and generalizes to 11 unseen representations, including pose skeletons, drawings, and point clouds.

Continual Concept Personalization in Diffusion Models via Residual-Space Adaptation

通过残差空间适配实现扩散模型中的持续概念个性化

方法: 作者将残差空间适配方法应用于预训练扩散模型的随机适配过程，使模型能够持续学习新概念（主体、物体或风格）而不干扰已学概念。通过学习到的残差空间动态分析适配容量在扩散模型中的分布位置，并据此限制适配层以提升参数效率。

Text-to-image personalization aims to equip pretrained diffusion models with the ability to learn user-specific concepts from only a few reference images. Existing methods, however, typically train each concept independently or assume that all concepts are available simultaneously. In practical applications, concepts often arrive sequentially, requiring models to continually learn new subjects, objects, or styles while minimizing interference with previously acquired concepts. We address this continual concept personalization setting by extending residual-space adaptation to the stochastic adaptation process of pretrained diffusion models. Our work achieves the highest concept fidelity among competing continual personalization methods while maintaining near-zero forgetting. Beyond these empirical gains, the learned residual-space dynamics reveal where adaptation capacity is concentrated within the diffusion model. Restricting adaptation to the identified layers by our method preserves nearly the same performance while reducing the number of trainable parameters by up to 72\%, demonstrating that residual-space dynamics can identify the layers that contribute most to continual concept personalization and directly guide more parameter-efficient adaptation.

Code-based video generation offers fine-grained control, targeted editing, and component reuse compared with direct video generation. This approach requires multimodal large language models (MLLMs) to translate visual and temporal requirements into executable programs that produce complete videos. However, existing code-based benchmarks primarily target specialized tasks, leaving complete video creation across diverse creative categories and input modalities insufficiently evaluated. We introduce Code2Video-Compass, a general-purpose benchmark for code-based video generation across text-to-video (T2V), image-to-video (I2V), and video-to-video (V2V) settings. Code2Video-Compass comprises 2,104 tasks spanning 10 categories, 24 subtypes, and three complexity dimensions covering construction, dynamic animation, and state dependency. It combines tasks curated from textual instructions and visual references with corresponding T2V, I2V, and V2V tasks derived from complete professional and user-created videos. We propose a dual-level evaluation framework combining 12 objective metrics and five subjective metrics. Objective evaluation covers operational performance, video quality, text-video alignment, and reference fidelity, while subjective evaluation uses an MLLM judge to evaluate instruction following and holistic video quality. Extensive evaluation of 15 recent proprietary and open-weight MLLMs from nine families reveals that even leading models face challenges in reliably generating high-quality complete videos aligned with textual and visual requirements, with performance generally declining as construction, dynamic animation, and state-dependency complexity increases. We anticipate that Code2Video-Compass will advance reliable, coherent, and controllable code-based video generation and inspire broader progress in programmatic visual creation.

AnyGameRenderer: Entity-Consistent Generative Rendering Across Games and an Entity-Level Benchmark

AnyGameRenderer：跨游戏实体一致的生成式渲染及实体级基准

核心贡献: 提出AnyGameRenderer，一种视频到视频的生成式渲染器，能将AI智能体生成的粗糙游戏画面转化为高质量视觉效果，同时严格保持布局、相机及每个实体的位置与时间一致性，并构建了实体级评测基准。

AI coding agents can now build small playable games in minutes, but the results usually look like programmer art: gray blockouts, colored geometric primitives, and flat semantic blobs. We present AnyGameRenderer, a video-to-video generative renderer that turns such footage into high-quality visuals with characters, motion detail, and effects. Throughout, it keeps the layout, camera, and every entity's position and timing intact. Our AnyGameRenderer generalizes across a wide range of genres that agents build, such as tower defense, auto-battlers, top-down survivors, turn-based battles, kart racing, and crowd runners. AnyGameRenderer takes plain video as its game-state input, with no complex representations such as engine G-buffers, per-object class labels, or low-poly proxies. Gray blockouts, colored primitives, semantic maps, and wireframes all work as input. It also preserves entity correspondence: group counts stay correct, and entities appear and disappear on the right frame. We implement AnyGameRenderer by LoRA fine-tuning MiniMax-H3 ref2va, a 33B reference-video-conditioned video generation model. Training uses $\sim$10k paired clips from three sources: (i) multi-stream rendering data from 38 agent-made games exported from GEAR, an AI game-creation tool; (ii) two-stream rendering data from Unreal Engine, with programmatically controlled crowds, count changes, and entity scales; and (iii) real gameplay recordings, with pairs reconstructed via 3D-box detection. To evaluate the entity correspondence, we propose an entity-level benchmark of 59 held-out cases with 753 entities, scored against ground-truth boxes. Compared with the base model, AnyGameRenderer better preserves entity correspondence: temporal match rises from 50.9\% to 62.9\%, missed count changes fall from 34.7\% to 21.3\%, and fewer entities are hallucinated or left unrendered.

Direct Forcing: A Single-Stage Training Recipe for Few-Step Autoregressive Text-to-Video Generation

直接强制：面向少步自回归文本到视频生成的单阶段训练配方

Haojun Xu, Jie Huang, Xin Lu, Mingchen Zhong, Zihao Fan 等 (7 位作者)

核心贡献: 提出Direct Forcing单阶段训练框架，将因果适配与分布匹配统一到一个训练阶段中，无需单独的初始化阶段或在线扩散分数监督，即可从双向预训练视频模型直接学习少步自回归视频生成器。
方法: 该方法将参考视频同时用作历史延续的配对样本和自生成rollout的目标分布。因果延续对齐（Causal Continuation Alignment）在参考历史条件下学习配对的时空对应关系；Rollout分布匹配（Rollout Distribution Matching）利用最大均值差异（MMD）对齐自生成视频与参考视频的表示分布。动态梯度平衡（Dynamic Gradient Balancing）在训练过程中自适应调整两种信号的相对梯度贡献。从双向Wan2.1-1.3B模型出发，仅用250次更新即学到四步自回归生成器。
关键发现: Direct Forcing在250次更新内实现四步自回归生成，取得84.63的VBench总分和86.01的质量分，且无需单独初始化阶段或在线扩散分数监督。消融实验验证了动态梯度平衡的有效性，并揭示了不同的表示偏好：更深层特征有利于因果延续对齐，而中间层特征对rollout分布匹配效果更佳。

Few-step autoregressive video generation enables low-latency streaming, yet prevailing training pipelines separate causal adaptation from distribution matching. Recent representation-based text-to-video methods retain this separation by starting from ODE-initialized checkpoints, even when the target distribution is defined directly by reference videos. We introduce Direct Forcing, a single-stage framework that uses reference videos both as paired examples of how a history continues and as a target population for self-generated rollouts. Causal Continuation Alignment learns paired spatiotemporal correspondence under reference histories, while Rollout Distribution Matching uses maximum mean discrepancy to align the representation distributions of self-generated and reference videos. Together, these complementary signals allow a pretrained video model to learn causal continuation and the target video distribution in a single training stage. Dynamic Gradient Balancing adapts the relative gradient contributions of continuation alignment and distribution matching during training. Starting from bidirectional Wan2.1-1.3B, Direct Forcing learns a four-step autoregressive generator in 250 updates without a separate initialization stage or online diffusion-score supervision, achieving 84.63 VBench Total and 86.01 Quality. Ablation studies further demonstrate the benefits of dynamic gradient balancing and reveal distinct representation preferences: deeper features benefit continuation alignment, whereas intermediate features yield better performance for rollout distribution matching.
