Four-Step Text-Bearing Image Generation via Distillation with Local Response Regularization and Teacher-Guided Training

基于局部响应正则化与教师引导训练的四步蒸馏文本图像生成

Kairui Wen, Sheng Ye, Yong-Jin Liu

核心贡献: 提出一种四步蒸馏框架，将局部响应正则化与教师引导的文本训练相结合，在显著降低采样成本的同时保持文本图像中场景的连贯性与字符的准确性。
方法: 该方法基于分布匹配，对GLM-Image DiT进行蒸馏，同时保留其自回归视觉先验、字形编码器和VAE。通过路径长度惩罚促进条件去噪场的局部响应一致性，并采用渐进式训练，将合成字符串与教师生成的图像相结合，以保持字符准确性和场景融合的排版效果。
关键发现: 学生模型在GenEval上取得0.84的总体得分，CVTG-2K词准确率为0.5045，而四步TDM分别为0.81和0.1690；系数研究显示10^-5较优，Qwen-Image实验表明响应正则化和文本监督可带来额外增益。单图推理耗时1.742秒，相比教师的5.818秒实现3.34倍加速，且GPU显存需求相同（40 GB）。

Generating text-bearing images requires coherent scenes and accurate characters. Large text-to-image models improve both capabilities through repeated denoising, at substantial sampling cost. Few-step distillation reduces this cost but requires the student to organize scenes and refine glyphs within a short trajectory. We present a four-step distillation framework combining local response regularization with teacher-guided text training. Built on distribution matching, the framework distills the GLM-Image DiT while retaining its autoregressive visual prior, glyph encoder, and VAE. A path-length penalty promotes consistent local responses of the conditional denoising field. Progressive training combines synthetic strings with teacher-generated imagery to preserve character accuracy and scene-integrated typography. The student achieves GenEval Overall 0.84 and CVTG-2K word accuracy 0.5045, compared with 0.81 and 0.1690 for four-step TDM. A coefficient study favors $10^{-5}$, and Qwen-Image experiments show additional gains from response regularization and text supervision. Single-image inference takes 1.742 s, compared with 5.818 s for the teacher, yielding a $3.34\times$ speedup at the same 40 GB GPU memory requirement.

MotionGraphicsBench: Evaluating Motion Graphics Generation with Programmatic Verification

MotionGraphicsBench：基于程序化验证的运动图形生成评估基准

Jiaju Ma, Ashvini Kumar Jindal, Chuhua Wang, Sumithra Bhakthavatsalam

核心贡献: 论文提出了MotionGraphicsBench，一个包含100个专家精选实例的基准，用于评估大语言模型根据结合空间、外观和时间要求的提示生成运动图形的能力，并通过确定性的、人类可读的验证程序而非参考动画或VLM评判来进行评估。
方法: 该基准基于真实世界的Lottie运动设计，每个实例将输入静态SVG和运动编辑提示与一个验证程序配对，该程序接受不同的有效实现并报告哪些提示要求通过或失败。验证程序通过多智能体流水线构建，使用人工标注的动画和合成测试用例。评估时，在180个动画的平衡测试集上，将程序的判断与人类判断进行比较，并测试了来自32个LLM和12个提供商的47种配置。
关键发现: 验证程序与人类判断的原始一致率达到93.3%，优于GPT-6 Astra VLM基线的83.3%，并在批准和拒绝召回率上均优于基线。在47种配置中，pass@1范围从6.0%到67.7%；在15个模型以最高推理努力和每实例32次尝试评估时，额外尝试能提升所有模型，但pass@32仍从38.0%到95.0%不等。分析表明，生成的动画经常无法匹配参考姿势、遵循请求的运动轨迹或协调相关事件。

Large language models can generate motion graphics from static images and natural language requests, but it remains difficult to evaluate how well models can follow requests that combine spatial, appearance, and temporal requirements. We introduce MotionGraphicsBench, an expert-curated benchmark of 100 instances for animating an input static SVG with a motion edit prompt. By supplying all required visual elements, our editing task reflects a designer-like workflow and isolates motion generation from graphic generation. Derived from real-world Lottie motion designs, our benchmark contains diverse combinations of spatio-temporal animation behaviors. Each benchmark instance pairs its model input with a deterministic, human-readable verification program that accepts different valid implementations and reports which prompt requirements pass or fail, rather than comparing against a reference animation or using a VLM judge. We construct these programs with a multi-agent pipeline using human-labeled animations and synthesized test cases. On a balanced test set of 180 animations, our programs achieve 93.3% raw agreement with human judgments, compared with 83.3% for a GPT-6 Astra VLM baseline, and outperform the baseline in both approval and rejection recall. Across 47 configurations from 32 LLMs and 12 providers, pass@1 ranges from 6.0% to 67.7%. Across 15 models evaluated at their highest available reasoning effort with 32 attempts per instance, additional attempts improve every model, but pass@32 still ranges from 38.0% to 95.0%. Our analysis of verification programs reveals that generated animations often fail to match reference poses, follow requested motion trajectories, or coordinate related events. We release our benchmark at https://huggingface.co/datasets/figma-ai/motion-graphics-bench.

Direct Trajectory Optimization for Inference-Time Alignment of Text-to-Image Diffusion Models

面向文本到图像扩散模型推理时对齐的直接轨迹优化

Huixuan Zhang, Xiaojun Wan

核心贡献: 提出了一种无需训练的直接轨迹优化框架（DTO），通过直接优化扩散模型的采样轨迹来实现与奖励函数的对齐，避免了通过大型去噪网络进行昂贵的反向传播。
方法: DTO将中间去噪分数和采样噪声作为优化变量，无需通过去噪网络计算梯度。为缓解分布外问题，引入奖励优化分数引导，在优化分数与预训练模型预测之间进行插值以稳定采样，并在约束分布下提供奖励提升的可证明理论保证。
关键发现: 在三个扩散模型和两个基准测试上，DTO均提升了 compat 对齐性能，优于现有的推理时对齐方法，同时保持高效率。结果表明轨迹级优化是实现高效且有效的扩散模型对齐的有前景方向。
查看原文摘要

Alignment of text-to-image diffusion models with reward functions is a key challenge in controllable generation. Existing approaches typically rely on optimizing model inputs or fine-tuning denoising networks, which requires computationally expensive backpropagation through large denoising models. In this work, we propose Direct Trajectory Optimization (DTO), a training-free framework that performs alignment by directly optimizing the sampling trajectory of diffusion models. Specifically, DTO treats intermediate denoising scores and sampled noise as the optimization variables, eliminating the need for gradients through the denoising network. To mitigate the out-of-distribution problem, we introduce Reward-Optimized Score Guidance, which interpolates between the optimized scores and the pretrained model predictions to stabilize sampling, while providing a provable theoretical guarantee of reward improvement under constrained distributions. Empirically, DTO improves alignment performance across three diffusion models on two benchmarks, outperforming existing inference-time alignment methods while maintaining high efficiency. These results suggest that trajectory-level optimization is a promising direction for efficient and effective diffusion model alignment.

What kills $v$-prediction? A Patch-wise PCA Perspective on Pixel-Space Flow Matching

是什么扼杀了v-预测？基于分块PCA视角的像素空间流匹配研究

Tianze Luo, Haofeng Huang, Yuan Yao

方法: 作者采用分块PCA对噪声目标进行分析，发现次要成分中的速度主要由原始噪声输入决定，需要深层骨干网络保留与主成分建模相竞争的信息，从而形成带宽瓶颈。通过受控实验和表示分析，进一步解释了x-预测的有效性来源：其参数化提供了原始输入旁路，且学习到的输入表示能自发集中于主方向。基于此分析，提出RiT架构，将轻量级原始条件解码器与输出侧捷径相结合，分离语义建模与原始状态传播。
关键发现: 研究发现v-预测的困难源于分块PCA下的带宽瓶颈：次要成分的速度信息依赖原始噪声输入，与主成分建模形成竞争。x-预测之所以有效，是因为其参数化提供了原始输入旁路，且学习表示自发聚焦主方向；在低秩观测下，x-预测可被刻画为隐式条件均值解码器，能从保留信息中重建被丢弃的成分。RiT方法成功恢复了有效的v-预测，并在ImageNet 256×256上达到有竞争力的性能，揭示了预测目标与架构带宽之间的交互关系。

Latent Diffusion Models dominate generative modeling but face two-stage training complexity and the inherent reconstruction limits of pre-trained autoencoders. Pixel-space Flow Matching offers a simplified end-to-end alternative yet often encounters training difficulties when using the standard $v$-prediction objective. Recent research attributes this difficulty to the off-manifold nature of noised targets, motivating a shift toward $x$-prediction. In this paper, we investigate this explanation through patch-wise Principal Component Analysis (PCA) and identify a bandwidth bottleneck. The velocity in minor components is largely determined by the raw noisy input, requiring deep backbones to preserve information that competes with principal-component modeling. Through controlled experiments and representation analysis, we further examine why $x$-prediction remains effective: its parameterization provides a raw-input bypass, while its learned input representation can spontaneously concentrate on principal directions. Under low-rank observations, our analysis of the induced flow characterizes $x$-prediction as an implicit conditional-mean decoder, reconstructing discarded components from the retained information. These observations motivate the Raw-injected Transformer (RiT), which combines a lightweight raw-conditioned decoder with an output-side shortcut to separate semantic modeling from raw-state propagation. Our method restores effective $v$-prediction and achieves competitive performance on ImageNet $256\times256$, providing a perspective on the interaction between prediction objectives and architectural bandwidth.

dNaViT: Discrete Codes are General-Purpose Visual Representations

dNaViT：离散码是通用视觉表示

核心贡献: 论文提出dNaViT，一种离散原生分辨率视觉Transformer，将视觉世界表示为类似语言token的离散视觉基元，证明了紧凑的离散词汇表可作为通用视觉货币，支撑统一的多模态方法。
方法: dNaViT基于冻结的预训练编码器，通过轻量级残差调制和双分支目标将重建感知线索注入语义特征；利用分组乘积量化扩展离散容量而不增加空间序列长度；采用平衡训练策略同时保留语义和像素级信息，从而作为通用的离散视觉分词器，统一编码下游任务并支持高保真图像解码。
关键发现: 在12个视觉理解基准、4个生成基准和1个图像编辑基准上，dNaViT匹配或超越了任务特定基线，表明紧凑的离散词汇表能够作为通用视觉表示，释放完全统一的原生多模态方法的潜力。

Discrete autoregressive modeling, notably through next-token prediction, has become a cornerstone of modern language models. Extending this paradigm to vision allows visual and language models to converge on a unified framework, thereby fostering shared methodology and infrastructure. This work investigates a central question: how far can a finite discrete vocabulary go in representing the richness of the visual world? We present dNaViT, a Discrete Native-resolution Vision Transformer that produces discrete visual primitives analogous to language tokens. Built upon a frozen pretrained encoder, dNaViT applies lightweight residual modulation with a dual-branch objective to inject reconstruction-aware cues into semantic features. By leveraging grouped product quantization, it scales discrete capacity without inflating spatial sequence lengths, while a balanced training recipe maintains both semantic and pixel-level information. Consequently, dNaViT functions as a versatile discrete visual tokenizer, enabling unified encoding across downstream tasks alongside high-fidelity image decoding. Extensive evaluations show that dNaViT matches or exceeds task-specific baselines across 12 visual understanding benchmarks, 4 generation benchmarks, and an image editing benchmark. These findings demonstrate that a compact discrete vocabulary can serve as a universal visual currency and unlock the full potential of fully unified, native multimodal methodology.
